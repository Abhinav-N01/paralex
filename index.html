Path of Funds

Trace every dollar from source to escrow

USCIS requires that the full $800,000 investment be traced dollar-by-dollar — from where it was earned, through each bank account it passed through, all the way to the regional center escrow account. ParaLex does this automatically by reading all the uploaded bank statements, wire records, and account documents.

⚠️ What USCIS looks for in Path of Funds:

  • Each account the funds passed through, with matching statements
  • No unexplained gaps — every large deposit must be explained
  • Wire transfer confirmations with exact amounts and dates
  • Foreign-to-US transfers must show currency conversion rates
  • 401(k) or stock liquidations must show the rollover/distribution

ParaLex queries the documents and instantly answers: "How did the money get from paycheck to escrow?" — with every step cited to a specific document and page.

Installment / Tranche Tracking

Investors don't always invest all at once

Many EB-5 regional centers structure the investment in tranches — an initial deposit to reserve a spot, then one or more capital calls as the project needs funding. ParaLex tracks every tranche, verifies each wire, and ensures the memo accounts for all of them.

How tranche investments work

1️⃣
Initial Reservation Deposit

Investor wires a smaller amount (e.g., $100,000) to hold their spot in the project. The subscription agreement is signed at this point.

2️⃣
Capital Call (Remaining Balance)

When the regional center is ready to deploy capital, they issue a "capital call." The investor wires the remaining amount (e.g., $700,000) to complete the $800,000 investment.

3️⃣
USCIS Filing After Full Investment

The I-526E petition is filed once the full amount is in escrow. The SOF memo must document every wire transfer and show how the total $800,000 was accumulated.

What ParaLex does: Automatically reads all wire transfer documents, identifies each tranche, totals them up, verifies the sum meets the TEA minimum ($800,000) or standard minimum ($1,050,000), and writes it into the fund tracing table in the SOF memo with each wire on a separate row with its own exhibit reference.

Document Requirements

Every document ParaLex handles

Optimized for Indian nationals on H-1B visa filing EB-5. Handles both US documents and Indian documents including PAN card, ITR, Form 16, and statements from HDFC, SBI, ICICI, and other Indian banks.

⚠️ 7-Year Bank Statement Rule

USCIS requires 7 consecutive years of monthly statements for every bank account — both US and foreign. Missing even a single month may trigger an RFE (Request for Evidence) that delays the petition by 12+ months.
